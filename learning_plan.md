# English Learning Plan — C1

**Plan start:** October 2026  
**Target:** reach a solid CEFR C1 level by July 2027.  
**Current level:** to be confirmed by an assessment; no certification or measured C1 baseline is assumed.

## Purpose

Develop confident, accurate and spontaneous English for executive communication and everyday interactions. Priority contexts: C-level interviews, discussions with boards, strategic disagreements, leadership, AI, data, products, credit, collections and fraud. Complement professional practice with conversations about travel, music, hobbies, films and current affairs.

**English contact:** 8 h 30 min/week = exercise 4 h 10 min + commute 2 h 30 min + music 50 min + private lesson 1 h. The full bilingual schedule includes Spanish 3 h 20 min/week (two 60-minute lessons, 30 minutes of Wednesday conversation and Duolingo 10 minutes Monday–Friday): **11 h 50 min combined**. These are planned contact hours, not equivalent hours of focused instruction. The commute time is an opportunity, not an obligation to speak continuously.

## Immersion material and use

- **Series — The Office (US):** Tuesday and Thursday, up to 50 minutes each. Original English audio; use English subtitles if needed. Focus on spontaneous exchanges, everyday language, workplace conflict and humor. Discuss an episode or scene later without presuming that every joke was understood. Confirm episode and spoiler boundaries before each discussion.
- **Podcast — WorkLab: The Podcast (Microsoft):** Monday, Wednesday and Friday, up to 50 minutes each. Prioritize episodes on AI, leadership and the future of work. Listen once for the general argument; replay relevant segments if training interruptions interfere. For later discussion, retrieve one claim, one example and one useful expression. Source: https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/worklab/podcast
- **Music:** 10 minutes every weekday in the shower. Select a song, examine its meaning and two useful expressions beforehand, then listen to familiar passages. Music is supplementary, not speaking practice.

## Conversation practice during commute

- **Monday (60 min available):** executive interview, case narrative, board challenge or handling objections.
- **Tuesday (60 min available):** casual conversation, travel, films and personal interests; occasionally discuss *The Office*.
- **Wednesday (30 min in English):** discuss a verified WorkLab episode, summarize the week, or retrieve useful expressions. The other 30 minutes are reserved for Spanish.
- Only voice interaction while driving. Pause or stop whenever traffic or concentration requires it; no reading, typing or visual tasks.
- Use one question at a time, allow spontaneous answers, then correct two or three observed issues after a short block and ask for a new attempt.

## Private teacher — Tuesday, 60 minutes

Align lesson content with the teacher. Possible rotation: executive simulations, precision/pronunciation, complex listening, negotiation and spontaneous conversation. Bring at most three observed difficulties or target expressions. The teacher's feedback is reported as such and not treated as an assessment performed by ChatGPT.

## Reading and writing

Add 10–15 minutes of focused reading/writing when feasible, separate from exercise or driving: a short article, a summary of an episode, a meeting note or a 120–200-word argument. Treat this as a proposed extra block, not part of the 8 h 30 min count.

## Learning milestones

| Period | Development focus |
|---|---|
| Oct–Dec 2026 | Baseline assessment; consolidate clarity, accuracy, fluency and listening gaps |
| Jan–Mar 2027 | More nuanced argumentation, collocations, spontaneous reformulation and sustained conversations |
| Apr–May 2027 | Executive interviews, board challenges, negotiations and handling pressure |
| Jun–Jul 2027 | Demonstrate consistent C1-like performance across speaking, listening, reading and writing; optional external certification |

Milestones are aspirations, not evidence of proficiency.

## Assessment and adjustment

- Obtain an initial diagnostic under focused conditions. Monthly reviews should include speaking/interaction, listening, reading and writing; record unassessed dimensions as such.
- Compare spontaneous performance across comparable tasks; do not infer listening or pronunciation from text transcripts.
- Review how much of the material is actually comprehended and how many expressions transfer into spontaneous speech. Adapt task difficulty before simply adding hours.
- Coordinate schedules with the Spanish project: Spanish lessons are Wednesday/Friday, plus Wednesday voice conversation and 10 minutes of Duolingo Monday–Friday.
